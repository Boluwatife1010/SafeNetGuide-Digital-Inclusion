# SafeNetGuide-Digital-Inclusion
SafeNet Guide

SafeNet Guide is a digital inclusion platform designed to help people in Nigeria learn how to use the internet safely and avoid online scams.

The platform provides simple cybersecurity lessons, scam detection awareness, and beginner digital skills tutorials.

Problem:
Many people entering the digital economy lack the knowledge to identify phishing attacks, fake job offers, and online fraud.

Solution:
SafeNet Guide provides easy-to-understand cybersecurity education and digital literacy tools that empower users to navigate the internet safely.

Features:
- Digital Safety Lessons
- Scam Checker
- Digital Skills Guide
- Cybersecurity Awareness Tips

- 

Impact:
This platform promotes digital inclusion by helping individuals safely participate in online services such as banking, job applications, and e-commerce.
